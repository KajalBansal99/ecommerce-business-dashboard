# E-commerce Business Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The E-commerce Business Dashboard is an interactive data visualization project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides an overview of e-commerce sales performance, customer orders, advertising performance, product categories, and marketing channels.

The dashboard helps users explore business data through visualizations and interactive filters.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and order performance.
- Monitor advertising spend and ad clicks.
- Compare sales across product categories and cities.
- Understand order status and customer types.
- Explore sales performance across marketing channels and payment methods.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Data Visualization
- Business Data Analysis
- Interactive Slicers and KPI Cards

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

- **Total Sales:** Overall sales amount.
- **Total Orders:** Total number of orders.
- **Advertising Spend:** Total amount spent on advertising.
- **Ad Clicks:** Total number of advertising clicks.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains the following charts:

1. **Monthly Sales Trend** — Tracks sales over time.
2. **Sales by Product Category** — Compares sales across product categories.
3. **Orders by Status** — Shows the distribution of order statuses.
4. **Sales by City** — Compares sales across cities.
5. **Sales by Marketing Channel** — Analyzes sales across marketing channels.
6. **Orders by Customer Type** — Compares orders by customer type.
7. **Sales by Payment Method** — Compares sales across payment methods.

## 🎛️ Interactive Features

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:

- City
- Product Category
- Order Status
- Marketing Channel

These filters allow users to explore the dashboard based on different business dimensions.

## 💡 Key Learnings

Through this project, I practised:

- Creating KPI cards and charts in Power BI.
- Using slicers to make reports interactive.
- Formatting and arranging dashboard visuals.
- Presenting business data in a clear and structured way.
- Exploring sales and marketing performance through visual analysis.
